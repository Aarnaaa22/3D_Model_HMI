01 — PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Meet Petal.

A non-technical product concept focused on everyday utility, simple interaction and a playful modular form.

01 / DETACHABLE MODULES
01

Organisation
with a softer shape.

The Petal organizer uses a circular central body with curved compartments arranged around it. Each compartment creates a dedicated space for lightweight everyday objects such as paper clips, pins, beads, erasers and other small stationery or craft supplies.

The separate components create a modular layout: users can see the individual sections, sort objects by category and access the items they need.

DESIGNED AROUND THE USER

Simple to see.
Simple to use.

The product applies human-centred design principles to a familiar, everyday task. Its open compartments make stored objects visible, while the repeated sections suggest where different items can be placed.

  • Visibility — contents can be seen at a glance.
  • Clarity — separate sections make sorting intuitive.
  • Accessibility — individual compartments are easy to approach.
  • Comfort — printed edges should be checked and smoothed before use.
