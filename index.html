01 / DETACHABLE PETAL CAPS
01

Highlighting
with a softer shape.

The Petal highlighter integrates five individual highlighter cartridges into a star-configured central body, each shielded by a removable petal-shaped cap. Each petal tip features a distinct vibrant hue (such as pink, yellow, blue, green, and orange), allowing students and readers to color-code notes without juggling five separate pens.

The modular caps snap firmly onto the central base, protecting the highlighter nibs from drying out while providing an easy-to-grip ergonomic petal shape when highlighting.

DESIGNED AROUND THE USER

Simple to see.
Delightful to use.

The multi-coloured highlighter applies human-centred interaction principles to everyday note-taking. Its distinct petal wings make colour selection instantaneous, while the compact floral form rests comfortably in the hand.

  • Colour Visibility — bright colour-coded petals make each highlighter shade immediately recognizable.
  • Speed & Flow — switch seamlessly between five colours while studying without fumbling for pens.
  • Ergonomic Comfort — rounded contours and petal wings provide natural finger rests during writing.
  • Airtight Seal — engineered snap-fit cap retention keeps inks fresh and ready for use.
